## 3. Authority and Reference Rules

1. **A reference is not authority.** A citation to a Part 0–14 section points the reader there; it does not make the citing document authoritative.
2. **A citation is not a decision.** Mentioning an ADR does not make its decision binding in a new scope.
3. **Part 15 cannot override Parts 0–14.** When Part 15 and Parts 0–14 disagree, Parts 0–14 govern.
4. **An ADR is authoritative only when its actual record and status support that conclusion.** Part 14 `adrs.md` is an *integration index* for ADRs; it is "non-authoritative for ADR decisions (source ADR documents are authoritative for their own decisions)" (Part 14 `adrs.md` §0).
5. **A cross-reference must preserve source authority.** 15.13 never upgrades a citation into architecture.
6. **Draft ADRs do not constrain implementation.** P13-ADR-001..010 are Draft per Part 13 `adrs.md`; they are PROPOSED only (Part 14 `adrs.md` Rule 0.3).
7. **No new ADR is created here.** No ADR ID, title, status, or decision is invented in this document.
