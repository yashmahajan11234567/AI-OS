This chapter translates Parts 0–14 memory, knowledge, state, context, and persistence architecture into implementation-facing requirements. It covers five interacting subsystems: Memory, Knowledge, State, Context, and Storage. The chapter is organized by subsystem architecture and, critically, by the integration boundaries between them — documenting who communicates, through what boundary, what contract applies, what state crosses, and what remains unspecified.

No storage backend, retrieval mechanism, retention policy, or security implementation is invented; unspecified items are preserved verbatim from source material.
